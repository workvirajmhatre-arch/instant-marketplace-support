# Request deletion of your Instant Marketplace account

Use this page for the **Instant Customer** and **Instant Service Provider** apps. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data by email, even if you have uninstalled the app or lost access. You do not need a GitHub account.

**[Email Instant support to request account deletion](mailto:work.viraj.mhatre@gmail.com?subject=Instant%20Marketplace%20account%20deletion)**

You can also copy **work.viraj.mhatre@gmail.com** into your email app. Use the subject **Instant Marketplace account deletion**.

## What to include

1. Tell us which app or apps you use and that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.
2. If possible, write from the email address linked to your Instant account. If you have lost access, tell us and give us an email address where we can reply.
3. Keep your request private. Leave out passwords, sign-in codes, identity documents and payment details. Use email for your request or follow-up.

## What happens next

Support reviews your request and verifies account ownership before taking action. We may contact you privately if we need more information. Sending the email starts the request. You can ask about its progress by replying to the same email thread.

Your request covers your account profile and associated personal data. Some transaction, tax, dispute or security records may need to be retained for a specific legal or operational reason. If this affects your request, you can ask which records are retained, why, and for how long. Email support if you have an open booking, refund or dispute so it can be considered during review.

## Other support

For questions about either app, email **work.viraj.mhatre@gmail.com** with a short description of what you need help with.
